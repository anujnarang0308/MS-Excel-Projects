📊 **Retail Performance & Profitability Diagnostic (Superstore Case Study)**

📌 Project Overview
This repository contains an end-to-end business intelligence and performance analysis of a nationwide retail operation (Superstore). Using transaction-level data across 9,994 orders, this project evaluates product category profitability, customer segment value, logistics efficiency, and the financial impact of discounting strategies. The analysis combines data processing in Microsoft Excel with executive summary storytelling in PowerPoint.  

- <a href="https://github.com/anujnarang0308/MS-Excel-Projects/blob/main/Retail-Performance-Diagnostic/Superstore%20Sales%20%26%20Profitability%20Analysis%20Answers.pptx">power point </a>

🔑 Key Business Insights & FindingsCategory Performance: Technology leads overall performance with ₹836,154.03 in sales and 17.40% profit margin. Office Supplies demonstrates equal efficiency (17.04% margin), whereas Furniture generates substantial sales (₹741,999.80) but severely lags in profitability with a 2.49% margin.   Discount Impact: Statistical correlation analysis reveals a strong negative correlation (-0.8645) between aggressive discounts and profit margins, proving that discounting practices in key regions erode overall profitability.   Underperforming Regions: Identified critical loss-making state clusters in the Central (Texas, Illinois), East (Ohio, Pennsylvania), and West (Colorado, Arizona) regions requiring operational intervention.   Customer Segment Analysis: While the Home Office segment yields the highest profit margin percentage (14.03%), the Consumer segment drives the highest absolute sales (₹1,161,401.35) and profit volume (₹134,119.21).
